    OPERA

    Open computational earth and climate science research

    • Grenoble, France

    Join us

    OPERA is a research group at the Institut des Géosciences de l’Environnement (IGE), Université Grenoble Alpes & CNRS, in Grenoble, France. We work on open computational earth and climate science — process-based models, data-driven and hybrid approaches, and the systems that tie them together.

    Open positions

    There are no open positions at the moment.

    We are always happy to hear from talented people: if you would like to do a PhD, a postdoc or an internship with us, get in touch with the team members whose research is closest to yours — spontaneous applications are welcome anytime. New positions are also expected to open soon, so watch this page.

    Practical information

    • recruitment for permanent positions at IGE goes through CNRS and Université Grenoble Alpes,
    • PhD positions are typically advertised in spring for a start in autumn,
    • internships (Master level) are often available — write to us with your CV and interests,
    • we support open science: your work with us will be public, reproducible and free.
